Learn how to make salmon data easier to understand, share, and reuse. This workshop provides **12 hours of teaching and activities over two days**, excluding breaks. Day 1 introduces data description, packaging, and a test catalog publication demonstration. Day 2 explores controlled vocabularies, ontology models, and connections to shared meanings. The dataset and exercise materials are provided.

On **Day 1, Chapters 1–7**, draw the relationships behind the observations, write and peer-review a data dictionary, build a [Salmon Data Package](glossary.html#salmon-data-package), and compare your mappings with recorded [AI](glossary.html#ai) suggestions. Then describe codes and unresolved terms, validate the package, export [EML](glossary.html#eml), and inspect the [KNB](glossary.html#knb) test publication artifacts. The public test record is still pending; the reference page records its actual status.

On **[Day 2, Chapters 8–12](session-8.html)**, return to the same graph and dictionary. Decide which meanings can be reused, which need local stewardship, and which might warrant a shared-term request. Build a small [SKOS](glossary.html#skos) vocabulary, formalize part of the model with [OWL](glossary.html#owl), create and assess a [bridge](glossary.html#bridge), and prepare a contribution for human review. These are teaching drafts, not new official DFO or Salmon Domain Ontology terms. The source interpretations remain pending Bruno and Tom's domain review.

Spreadsheet, R, and Python learners work with the same source, worksheets, and package checkpoints. The required AI comparison uses supplied outputs; live AI calls are optional and no paid service or account is required for the core activities.

Start with [Setup](setup.html) and the [downloadable workshop kit](files/fraser-coho-workshop.zip). Keep the [Glossary](glossary.html) and [Field reference](field-reference.html) open as you work. The [Reference page](reference.html#teaching-record) records the teaching catalog's status and the distinction between technical checks, domain review, and publication authority.

Attend Day 1 alone, continue through both days, or use the full modular material for longer practice. Chapter 1 also works as a **55-minute overview**. The [instructor schedule](instructor-notes.html) gives each day's route; the [Day 2 guide](advanced.html) explains its prerequisites and outputs.
