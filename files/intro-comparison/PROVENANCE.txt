Chapter 1 comparison excerpts

These files support the introductory Yukon–Fraser comparison. They are
separate from the Fraser Coho NuSEDS teaching kit and its SDP checkpoints.

yukon-excerpt.csv retains all six source columns for stock
NorthernYukonR.andtribs., years 2022 and 2023, from:
https://github.com/Pacific-salmon-assess/Yukon-Chinook-CU-reconstructions/blob/3390d4f2a706e1923785aef24e96eba17a7002c0/data/generated/2025/esc-data.csv

fraser-excerpt.csv retains all 37 source columns for CU_ID CK-03, years
2022 and 2023, from:
https://github.com/sos-program/Approved-WSP-Statuses/blob/05643cf7474e2c468e21e30edbf1da0ef9a060a8/OUTPUT/Retrospective_RapidStatus_Results_Approved.csv

Selection preserves field names, parsed values and row order. The excerpts
are serialized locally with LF line endings. source-manifest.json records
the selection criteria and SHA-256 checksums of the full fetched sources
and local excerpts, with links to each source repository's license. Retain
the source attribution and consult those licenses when reusing the excerpts.

These are different CUs. The records do not establish a common biological
scope, interchangeable methods, a stock-to-CU crosswalk, or an approved
semantic mapping. Chapter 1's prepared description table is a teaching
preview and keeps the remaining questions visible. It is not canonical
SDP metadata. Domain review and selection of the final examples remain open.

linked to English et al. (2018). No Central Coast extract or newly calculated
CU estimate is supplied here. The hands-on exercises use the separate
Fraser Coho NuSEDS slice declared in the workshop setup.
