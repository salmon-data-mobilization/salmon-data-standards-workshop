## Purpose and scope

Help workshop participants connect four observed ESTIMATE_METHOD strings to
source-backed concept records. The selection covers Area Under the Curve,
Combined Methods, Fixed Site Census and Resistivity Counter from the unchanged
Fraser Coho source. It is not the full nine-value method inventory, an official
NuSEDS vocabulary or proof that the resulting estimates are comparable.

The three working definitions paraphrase the current official dictionary. Its
Resistivity Counter entry supplies no operational definition. The retrieval
date is 2026-09-08; applicability to the 2025 workbook remains a source-version
question. Existing DFO and shared resources must be checked before deciding
whether a production vocabulary is needed. The classroom creates a local draft
to learn the representation, not to establish that a new official scheme is missing.

## Proposed maintenance rules

- Keep the draft status and source evidence visible. Record actual reviews and
  decisions separately from who authored the artifact.
- Record spelling/citation corrections in a change log while preserving the
  term's intended meaning. Do not add unverified alternative labels.
- Review a changed scope as a semantic change. Decide explicitly whether a new
  concept or deprecation/replacement is required; do not silently reinterpret
  identifiers used by older data.
- Keep retrievable versions of adopted definitions and their source/review
  records. The organization must choose its version and release policy.
- Require a source check, scientific interpretation review, technical checks
  and an explicit adoption decision before a production release.
- Resolve or deliberately retain each definition gap according to that policy.
  Classroom completion with a documented gap does not establish release readiness.

## Worked change scenario

A maintainer receives a procedure source for Resistivity Counter. This is a
future evidence scenario, not a claim that the source has been obtained.

Before adding a definition, check its date, population/program coverage and
relationship to the actual estimate method. Ask whether it explains the same
category or introduces a narrower variant. Review any affected bridge separately.
Retain the earlier missing-definition record in the version history. The source
custodian and adopting steward decide the interpretation and release; a workshop
participant's edit alone is not that decision.
